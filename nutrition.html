Seven building blocks your body relies on

What each one does, and where to find it in everyday food.

P

Protein

Builds and repairs muscle, skin and other tissue.

Chicken, eggs, lentils, tofu
C

Carbohydrates

Your body's main and most efficient source of energy.

Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit
F

Healthy fats

Supports brain function and helps absorb key vitamins.

Avocado, olive oil, nuts
V

Vitamins

Supports immunity, vision and healthy skin.

Citrus, spinach, carrots
M

Minerals

Keeps bones, nerves and muscles working properly.

Dairy, leafy greens, seeds
Fi

Fibre

Supports digestion and keeps you full for longer.

Beans, oats, wholegrains
W

Water

Essential for nearly every process in the body.

Water, herbal tea, fruit
